AI Awareness Short Film

Give It a Little Distance · A Few Steps Away. A Lot of Respect.

Final Strategic Summary

AreaDevelopment
ProblemNon-smokers are often forced to move away from someone else’s smoke
Human insightSmokers may not intend harm; they may simply fail to notice who is affected
Behavioural solutionWhen possible, the smoker moves to a designated area or creates sufficient distance
Central ideaA small physical distance can communicate significant human respect
Campaign platformGive It a Little Distance
Supporting lineA Few Steps Away. A Lot of Respect.
Story deviceThe smoker notices a mother protecting her baby and chooses to move
Emotional resolutionAwareness leads to voluntary, respectful action
Communication toneHuman, positive, restrained and non-judgmental
Creative directionDialogue-free storytelling through smoke, distance, gesture and movement
AI workflowClaude + ChatGPT → Nano Banana → Seedance 2.5 → Adobe Premiere Pro
Key production disciplineCharacter-action continuity and edit-aware shot generation

Why This Case Study Matters

The campaign-production method strengthens this presentation by making the behavioural strategy, film language, AI prompting, continuity supervision and post-production process part of one coherent creative case study.
