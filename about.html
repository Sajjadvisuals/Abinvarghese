Abin Varghese

My creative journey began in Mumbai, after earning my master’s in Communication & Advertising from Mumbai University. Working across advertising agencies and production houses, I built my foundation behind the camera and inside the edit suite, as a videographer, editor and content creator. From television commercials and social campaigns to web series and feature-film projects, those formative years taught me how ideas move from script to screen.

For the past nine years, the UAE has been my creative ground. I have collaborated with leading agencies and production houses, shaping brand, campaign and broadcast content for some of the region’s most demanding clients.

Today, I bring together strategic thinking, filmmaking experience and emerging AI-production workflows, developing ideas, directing visuals and crafting stories from the first creative thought to the final frame.
